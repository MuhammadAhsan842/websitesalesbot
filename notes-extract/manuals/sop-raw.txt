  2. DISPATCHER ROLE & CORE OPERATIONAL MANDATE
   Load Board Sourcing: Continuously monitor load boards (DAT, Truckstop) to locate suitable,
   high-paying freight matching carrier equipment specifications.
   Rate Negotiation: Negotiate load rates directly with brokers to maximize gross revenue per mile
   for the carrier.
   Document Handling: Execute Carrier-Broker agreements, Rate Confirmations (Rate-Cons), and
   submit required onboarding packets.
   Payment Regulation & Safeguards: Brokers issue payments strictly and exclusively into the Motor
   Carrier (MC) owner's official bank account. Dispatchers never receive direct freight payments.

  3. CARRIER QUALIFICATION & ONBOARDING CHECKLIST
Before dispatching loads, verify and collect all required carrier compliance documentation:

  MANDATORY CARRIER DOCUMENT CHECKLIST
  1. Valid Driver's License: Commercial Driver's License (CDL) or standard driver's license
     matching equipment requirements.
  2. Certificate of Insurance (COI): Must list company name and address, with minimum
     coverage:
       Auto Liability: $750,000 to $1,000,000 coverage.
       Cargo Insurance: $100,000 cargo coverage required.
       Certificate Holder: Dispatch company / company address listed as holder.
  3. Truck Registration Sheet: Official state registration for all active trucks/trailers.
  4. Equipment Photos: Clear photos of the truck, trailer, and accessories.
  5. Notice of Assignment (NOA): Required if carrier uses a factoring company for payment
     processing.
