# Freight Dispatch & Carrier Onboarding Guide
**Standard Operating Procedures, Onboarding Requirements, Equipment Specifications, and Payment Structures**

---

## 1. Symmetry Conversation & Onboarding Workflow

### Core Conversation & Qualification Steps
1. **Greeting:** Establish professional rapport and introduce dispatch services.
2. **Type of Truck:** Determine equipment classification (Box Truck, Hot Shot, Semi Truck/Power Only, Dry Van, Reefer, Flatbed, Step Deck).
3. **Accessories of Truck:** Verify availability of loading/securing gear (Lift Gate, Pallet Jack, Straps, Tarps, Chains, Binders).
4. **Motor Carrier (MC) Authority Check:**
   * Request and verify MC Number.
   * **Age Requirement:** Target MC age is ideally **4–5 months** old minimum.
   * **Insurance Verification (If No MC / New MC):**
     * **Non-Trucking Liability**
     * **Automobile Coverage:** $750,000 to $1,000,000 auto liability coverage.
     * **Cargo Coverage:** $100,000 cargo insurance coverage.
     * *Note:* Ask carrier to add cargo coverage if auto liability is active but cargo is missing.

---

## 2. Dispatcher Sales Pitch & Commission Negotiation

### Case Study / Script Guidelines (e.g., Sales Scenario: "Jose")
* **Situation:** Prospect was previously approached and promised to follow up in 2 days. Sales manager followed up to inquire about current broker rates/charges.
* **Competitor Offer:** Prospect mentions receiving an **8% rate/charge on MC**.
* **Closing / Pitching Strategy:**
  * Convince and close at **10% commission**.
  * **Value Proposition / Earnings Target:** Target consistent gross weekly earnings of **$5,000 or more**, or **$6,000+ for Over-The-Road (OTR)** setups.

---

## 3. Operational Preferences & Route Setup

* **OTR vs. Local Load:**
  * **OTR (Over The Road):** Long distance, all over the country.
  * **Local Load:** Small miles / local radius.
* **Capacity & Schedule Questions:**
  * *How many miles can you cover in a day?*
  * *How long can you stay on the road?*
  * *Schedule:* **Monday to Friday** is standard and fine.
* **Expectation Management:**
  * Daily home time ("Back home everyday") is **not provided**.
  * Drivers sleep in the vehicle/truck at night during the workweek.
  * Focus is placed on securing the most **profitable loads** during OTR runs.

---

## 4. Onboarding Process & Document Checklist

### Onboarding Timeline & Workflow
1. Inquire: *"When would you like to start working?"*
2. Explain: Short and streamlined onboarding process.
3. Once all required documents are received, carrier profile is shifted to the **Dispatch Department**, where dispatchers immediately begin searching for and booking loads.

### Required Carrier Documents
1. **Driver's License**
2. **Certificate of Insurance (COI):** Listing company address and certificate holder details ($1,000,000 Auto Liability / $100,000 Cargo).
3. **Truck Registration Sheet**
4. **Photos of Truck / Equipment**
5. **Notice of Assignment (NOA):** Required if carrier uses a factoring company.

## 6. Payment Models & Financial Structures

### A. Dispatcher Pricing Models (Charging the Carrier)
1. **Flat Rate Fee:**
   * **Box Truck:** $250 – $300 fixed fee per week.
   * **Hot Shot:** $250 – $300 fixed fee per week.
   * **Semi Truck / Power Only:** $300 – $350 fixed fee per week.
2. **Percentage / Commission Rate:**
   * **Box Truck & Hot Shot:** 8% to 10% per load.
   * **Semi Trucks / Power Only:** 8% to 10% per load.

---

### B. Broker Payment Methods (How Carriers Get Paid)

1. **Standard Pay:**
   * **Timeline:** 24 to 28 days processing time.
   * **Deduction:** 0% fee (Full payment).

2. **Quick Pay:**
   * **Timeline:** 3 to 4 days processing time.
   * **Deduction:** 3% to 4% fee deducted by broker.
   * **Requirement:** Requires submitting a voided check along with rate confirmation and proof of delivery (BOL).

3. **Factoring Company:**
   * **Timeline:** Within 24 hours.
   * **Deduction:** 5% to 6% fee (handled via 3rd party factoring firm).
   * **Requirement:** **Notice of Assignment (NOA)** must be on file.

4. **Fuel Advances / Cash Advances:**
   * Direct fuel cards are **not provided**.
   * Monetary payment/advance is issued within **24 to 48 hours** to maintain high service standards and carrier satisfaction.
